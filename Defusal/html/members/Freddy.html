Welcome to My Personal Space!

Here you'll read and find more about me...

Hello there! I'm Freddy, a 19-year-old sophomore undergraduate student pursuing Computer Science and Technology at the Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT). Having grown up in Bandung, Indonesia, I've always admired the ability of technology to connect people and solve complicated issues across the world.

Throughout the years of my studies, my view on what can be done in computer science has changed greatly. Beyond basic syntax and algorithms, I have gained an incredible respect for the power of software engineering in creating our modern infrastructure, conducting research, and transforming industries.

Areas of Focus & Technical Interests

Although computer science has many connections with new frontiers of science, the areas I'm mostly interested in include:

  • Software Engineering & Architecture: Creating efficient, scalable solutions with elegant design.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Investigating the capabilities of intelligent systems to automate processes and assist humans in decision making.
  • Problem Solving & Algorithms Design: Improving efficiency and solving complex problems.

Vision & Future Goals

Technological changes are occurring at an unprecedented rate, and I wish to contribute to these changes. In the future, my plan is to use my theoretical and practical education to develop software systems that will help further technological progress and make people’s lives better by connecting technologies from Southeast Asia with the rest of the world.

Using this site, I hope to share the basic information about the field as well as document my current projects. I invite all other programmers, developers, and technology lovers to join me on this journey!
